Privacy Policy

Instagram comment giveaway · Last updated October 5, 2026

Comment giveaway is a free tool that helps pick winners among the comments of an Instagram post. This page explains which data is used and how.

What data we access

Only after you log in with Instagram and authorize it, the tool accesses:

  • the username of the account that logged in;
  • that account's posts and their comments (commenter username, text and date), only for the post whose link you provide.

How we use it

Comments are used exclusively to build the participant list, apply the rules you define and run the draw. All of this processing happens in your browser.

What we store

  • We keep no database and do not store comments, profiles or draw results on any server.
  • The Instagram access token is kept only in your browser session and is deleted when you click Log out or close the tab.
  • The login server only exchanges Instagram's authorization code for the token and returns it to your browser, without saving anything.
  • The draw rules are saved only in your browser, for your convenience.

Sharing

We do not sell or share data with third parties. The only external communication is with the official Instagram (Meta) API, required to fetch the comments.

Data deletion

Since we do not store your data, there is nothing to delete on our side. To end access:

  • click Log out in the tool;
  • on Instagram, go to Settings → Apps and websites and remove the "Sorteio Ins" app to revoke the authorization.

Contact

Privacy questions or requests can be sent through the project page: github.com/lucass-schneider/sorteio-instagram.
